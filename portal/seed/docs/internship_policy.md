# College Internship Policy (2026)

- Students from semester 5 onwards may take up internships.
- Minimum attendance of 75% is mandatory to receive a No Objection Certificate (NOC).
- Students may apply directly to companies. Before joining, the Department Internship
  Coordinator must approve the offer and issue an NOC.
- An industry mentor is assigned to every intern and verifies weekly logs.
- Students must log at least 30 hours per week and submit a weekly log every Friday.
- At the end, the student submits a final report; the mentor gives an overall rating (0-10).
- Final grade (100 marks): weekly consistency 25, technical depth 30, mentor evaluation 25,
  report & presentation 20. S >= 90, A >= 80, B >= 70, C >= 60, D >= 50, else F.
- The final grade is published only after the coordinator signs it off.
- Paying any fee to an employer for an internship is not allowed.
